Hello, travelers of the Silk Road! We are proud to present Ruta Dorada, a private Silkroad Online server built for relaxed play, where every level counts and there is far more to do than just leveling: Solo Leveling-style gates, hunter ranks, unique monsters with a live damage table, daily rewards, minigames and much more.

A server that grows with you: Ruta Dorada starts at Cap 80, but that is only the starting point, not the final level. V

Gates, Solo Leveling style

Dungeons that open at fixed times. Each one has a rank from E to S. Whoever finishes earns coins and rank points, and the fastest takes the loot.

When and how to enter

  • Schedule: 14:30, 17:30, 20:30 and 22:30 (server time, UTC-5). A few minutes before, the “Portal appearing” notice is shown.
  • Entry: through the teleporter in Jangan, Hotan or Constantinople, choosing “Gate E” … “Gate S (red)”. No mount, no caravan, no PVP cape and no job suit.
  • Always useful: the system only spawns gates of ranks that at least one hunter can use.

The gate takes you to the dungeon for your level bracket: 24–31, 32–41, 42–51, 52–63, 64–70 and 71–80. Below 24 you go to the first one. Gate S has its own dungeon.

Inside: a 15-minute race against the clock

  • First you clear the horde of 30 monsters, then you kill the gate's unique (two of them in some brackets). While it lives, it summons 4 elites every 30 seconds.
  • You compete against players in your bracket and your category: Strength (90% or more of your points in STR), Magic (90% or more in INT) or Hybrid.
  • Rewards: everyone who finishes earns coins (E 2 · D 3 · C 4 · B 5 · A 6 · S 8) and rank points. The best time in each category gets the loot, 50% more points and 1.5x the coins. Everything is delivered to the Item Storage Box.
  • Careful: some gates are traps (they hide a higher rank and pay out as such). The red gate is sealed: you cannot leave with a scroll until its boss falls. If nobody closes it in time there is a breach: the monsters spill out into the world.

Hunter rank, from E to S

Every character has a rank shown next to the name (“Name [D]”) and a title with bonuses. Everyone starts at E. Your rank goes up and down depending on your Power.

  • Power (0 to 100), recalculated every hour from 5 pillars: Level 15%, Gear 30%, Achievements and titles 20%, Events in the last 30 days 20% and Gates 15%.
  • Automatic promotion once an hour, once you meet every requirement. The seal and the party coins are deducted when you log out.

VII

Combativity coins

There are two kinds: solo and party. Both can be traded between players. Party coins are also required to promote to B, A and S.

How to earn them

  • Finishing gates (2 to 8 depending on the rank).
  • Hunting uniques: starting with Captain Ivy they also drop party coins.
  • In El Bruto and, once in a while, from any monster.
  • Exchange at the Hunter Association: 2 Arena Coins for 1 coin.

What they are spent on

  • Hunter Seal to promote: D 15 · C 40 · B 80 · A 160 · S 320.
  • Premium Magic POP Card: 25.
  • Low Quality Shining Stone: 5.
  • Entry to the Chamber of Vicious Shadows: 8 party coins.

IX

Events and systems

  • Magic POP: with a Magic POP Card (normal or Premium), play the machine in town and choose your prize. If you win, you get a red coupon for that prize; if not, a green coupon for a lesser one. Coupons are redeemed with Magic POP Guide Gori. When someone wins, the whole server finds out.
  • Daily reward: once per day per account: Daily Login notice (next to the Facebook one) → Claim, or at larutadorada.com/recompensas. The week advances from day 1 to day 7 and day 7 brings the best reward; it resets on Monday.
  • El Bruto: automatic fights in the portal with your real characters (stats, gear and skills): against players, gladiators and uniques. Missions, tournaments and a Power ranking. From level 25, with a weapon and 4 armor pieces; each skill requires its weapon.
  • Arcade: four minigames in the portal: Forge, Dragon Rain, Golden Caravan and Unique Hunter. They cost Silk and reward only items.
  • Muhan Tower: Battle of Infinity 75, waves to defend against, for levels 71–80, solo or in party, 2 entries per day.
  • Job crafting: 14 thief and hunter recipes, using pearls or stones, to craft job gear.
  • Stronger fellows: triple the STR, INT and health of the original, all 4 skill book slots open from level 1 and more speed.
  • Auto-potion with Vigor: in the macro (T key) there is a Vigor row: it is used automatically when your HP or MP drops below the percentage you choose.
  • Sort button: sorts your inventory with one click (or by typing .ordenar in chat): gear, potions, scrolls and the rest, each kept together.
X

Tips to get started

  • Press H whenever you have questions: the Ruta Dorada category explains gates, ranks, coins and rewards.
  • Claim the daily reward every day: the week only reaches day 7 if you do not miss a day.
  • Enter gates through the teleporter in Jangan, Hotan or Constantinople, not by looking for the portal on the map. The spot shown in the notice is where the breach will happen.
  • Buy the Hunter Seal before you meet the requirements: without the seal you will not rank up, even with the Power and level.
  • To promote to B, A and S, save party coins: they are deducted when you log out.
  • Your pet picks up Specialty Goods Boxes if you choose “Grab all item” in its filter.
  • Spend your stat points with purpose: in gates you compete against your own category (Strength, Magic or Hybrid).
  • Hunt uniques at your level: the ones that show up blue for you today drop nothing.
  • The Astral and Immortal stones from Magic POP are bound to you: they cannot be traded.
